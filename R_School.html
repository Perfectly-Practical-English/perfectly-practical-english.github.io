It’s about being responsible, respectful, and engaged in the classroom community. When students follow these ideas, they help create a positive environment for all. Positive Behavior Positive behavior means showing respect, responsibility, and care for the class. This includes being on time, ready to learn, and doing your best. When everyone behaves well, the classroom becomes a space where all can succeed. Punctuality Punctuality means arriving on time. It shows respect for teachers and classmates. When you're on time, you don’t miss key lessons and help keep the class on schedule. Preparedness Being prepared means bringing the materials you need, like books, notebooks, and pens. It helps you stay focused and shows you value learning. Participation, Attentiveness, and Focus Active participation includes listening, asking questions, and sharing ideas. Staying attentive and focused helps you learn and shows respect for the teacher and your peers. Productivity and Completion of Work Being productive means finishing work on time and putting in effort. It shows responsibility and helps keep the class on track. Polite, Respectful, and Cooperative Behavior Being polite and respectful means treating others kindly. Cooperation means working together and helping each other, creating a friendly atmosphere where everyone feels valued. Preservation of Facilities and Resources Caring for the classroom means not wasting materials, cleaning up, and respecting the space. This teaches responsibility and care for shared environments. Providing a Safe, Secure Environment A safe classroom is one where everyone feels protected and comfortable. This means avoiding bullying and following rules to keep everyone safe and focused on learning. Conclusion By following these rules, students become better learners and citizens. They learn to work with others, respect their environment, and prepare for their future roles in society. The goal is to encourage positive behavior while addressing challenges through guidance, mentoring, and support, rather than solely consequences. While supportive in nature, certain expectations for behavior remain essential and non-negotiable to maintain an effective learning environment. Core Principles • Behavior is treated as an opportunity for growth and development • Interventions are supportive, respectful, and non-confrontational • Expectations are applied consistently and fairly • Coaching is considered a normal part of student development, not a punishment • Core Values The framework is based on values that support learning and personal success: • Punctuality • Preparedness • Participation • Productivity • Respectful behavior • Care for resources and environment • Safety and well-being Behavior Expectations Positive Behaviors Students are expected to: • Arrive on time and attend all sessions • Come prepared with necessary materials • Stay engaged, attentive, and focused • Complete assigned work • Show respect and cooperate with others • Take care of shared spaces and resources • Contribute to a safe and supportive environment Negative / Unproductive Behaviors Behaviors that hinder learning include: • Tardiness or absence without reason • Lack of preparation • Distractions (e.g., off-task activity, excessive talking, unauthorized device use) • Incomplete work or academic dishonesty • Disrespectful or disruptive conduct • Misuse or damage of property and facilities • Unsafe behavior (e.g., bullying, fighting, inappropriate physical contact) Process for Addressing Behavior 1.
