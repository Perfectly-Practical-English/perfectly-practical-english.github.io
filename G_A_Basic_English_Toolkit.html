on, at, in, before, after, since, until, during, for, now, yesterday, today, tomorrow, later, then, when, while, once • Addition ➕ – words that add ideas together

because, so, since, because of, due to, as a result, therefore, that’s why • Comparison / Similarity / Difference ⚖️ – words that compare things
like, as, as...as, than, similar to, different from, more than, less than, the most, just as...so, whereas, in contrast to • Contrast / Opposition 🎭 – words that show differences
but, although, though, even though, however, yet, still, despite, instead, whereas, while, on the other hand • Frequency 〰️ – words that tell how often something happens
always, usually, often, sometimes, occasionally, rarely, never, every day, once a week, hardly ever, from time to time • Possibility / Certainty 🎲 – words that show how possible or sure something is
maybe, probably, possibly, must, might, may, could, can, definitely, certainly • Advice / Obligation / Permission ☝️ – words that tell rules or give guidance
must, must not, have to, don’t have to, should, ought to, had better, can, cannot, may, could • Manner 🎭 – words that tell how something is done

only, just, even, especially, mainly, particularly • Questions (Interrogatives) ❓ – words used to ask questions
